Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers the Android game Zippy's Acorn Dash (package com.zippys.acorndash).

What we collect

  • Game progress: scores, acorns, diamonds, unlocked skins, missions and settings, stored on your device and, if you sign in, in our cloud database (Google Firebase).
  • Account info: if you choose Google Sign-In, your name, email address and profile picture are provided to us by Google to identify your save and show your name on the leaderboard.
  • Leaderboard data: your display name and best scores are visible to other players.
  • Usage and device data: basic analytics and crash information, and, through advertising, your device's advertising ID and approximate location.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may use your advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policy and manage ad personalisation in your Android settings. You can remove ads with the in-app Remove Ads purchase.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Third-party services

Google Play Services, Google AdMob, Google Sign-In and Firebase each have their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is family friendly but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and data deletion

You can play without signing in. To request deletion of your cloud data, email us from the address linked to your account.
